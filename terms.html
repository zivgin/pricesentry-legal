2. Description of Service

PriceSentry is a browser extension that helps you track product prices across a range of online stores. It currently supports:

  • Amazon, across 16 international marketplaces including Amazon.ae (UAE), .com, .co.uk, .de, .fr, .it, .es, .ca, .com.au, .co.jp, .in, .com.br, .com.mx, .nl, .se and .sa
  • Sharaf DG, Zara, H&M, IKEA, Home Centre, Kibsons, Brands For Less, and 6th Street (including its aivi.com storefront)

With PriceSentry you can:

  • Track product prices and view price history charts and trends
  • Receive price drop alerts and notifications (including back-in-stock alerts)
  • See a community deal feed of trending price drops other users are catching
  • Import your Amazon wishlist to track items in bulk
  • Optionally sync your data across devices via cloud storage
  • Share deals with friends via WhatsApp, Telegram, a copied link, or email
  • Earn achievement badges

The list of supported stores and features may grow or change over time.
